Training Tracker

Training Tracker shows who in your department has not done the University's compulsory training, and sends them a reminder.

It is a browser extension for Chrome and Edge, for University of Oxford departments. It follows the six courses every member of staff must do, and Research Integrity for researchers. Everything is kept in one file, in a folder you choose.
