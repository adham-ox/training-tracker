Training Tracker

Support

Help with Training Tracker, a browser extension for Chrome and Edge that shows who in a department has not done the University's compulsory training, and sends them a reminder.

Common problems

The tracker asks me to reconnect its folder

The browser asks again for permission to use a folder after it restarts. Press Reconnect folder and allow it. Nothing has been lost.

My import is refused

The tracker checks the file before it saves anything, and says why it refused it. The usual reasons:

  • one of the seven compulsory courses was not selected in Power BI before the export;
  • the file is for another department, or is a different report;
  • Power BI cut the file off because it was too large. Select only the seven courses and export again.

I cannot send

Sending needs you to sign in with your University account, in Settings › Sending. To send from a shared address, you must already be allowed to send from it in Outlook. Before anything is sent, the tracker lists every address and asks you to type the word send.

A colleague and I both use the tracker

Only one person should use a tracker at a time. Keep it in a shared folder, such as your department's SharePoint, and close it when you have finished so a colleague can open it.

The tracker says it was saved by a newer version

A colleague has a newer version of Training Tracker. Update yours, then open the tracker again.

Reporting a problem

  1. In the tracker, open Help and choose Report a problem.
  2. Say what you were doing and what happened. Do not name people or paste training records.
  3. Press Show what will be sent and read it. The tracker hides the names and addresses it knows.
  4. Press Copy the report, and paste it into an email to the contact address on the extension's Chrome Web Store page, or to whoever looks after the tracker in your department.

The tracker sends nothing by itself.

Your data

Everything the tracker holds is in one file, in the folder your department chose, and in your own browser. The privacy statement says what is held and for how long.
